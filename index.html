PAGE 05 / ABOUT

A site for people who actually want to remember what they study.

KOKO combines chess coaching with the logic of a notebook: isolate the idea, write it down, test yourself, come back later.

The aim is practical improvement rather than passive consumption—whether you are learning your first opening ideas, reviewing your own games or using Anki to build long-term memory.
