Learn languages, chess, maths and school subjects — and remember them.

KOKO combines Anki flashcards and chess coaching with the logic of a notebook: isolate the idea, write it down, test yourself, come back later. The deck library covers languages, chess, maths and school subjects.

The aim is practical improvement rather than passive consumption—whether you are learning a language, mastering chess patterns, working through maths or revising school subjects.
